Ticket handoff contract: a concrete decision from the original ticket owner is a structured attention request, not routine progress.
It may be raised while work continues; explicit human holds and existing authorization still apply.
A question raised by a person's message is asked where that message was written, and any other question in the original ticket conversation.
Routine progress, model retries and budget errors alone do not authorize a message.
Do not change models or retry policy to conceal a failure.
A brief final confirmation word such as Done preserves a successful report; do not repeat the full report in final, and never emit an empty response.
